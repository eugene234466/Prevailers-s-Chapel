About Us

Prevailers Chapel International is a vibrant community of believers committed to experiencing God's transforming power and sharing His love with the world. We are a family where everyone belongs, growing together in faith, worship, and service. We passionately prioritize soul-winning and spiritual growth.

Our mission is to empower people to discover their divine purpose, develop their faith, and make a lasting difference in their communities. Through dynamic worship, biblical teaching, and genuine fellowship, we create an atmosphere where lives are changed and destinies are fulfilled.
